About the Work

Outside of brand and client work, I'm an independent filmmaker and commercial director with more than two decades behind the camera, producing, directing, shooting, and editing commercials, short films, music videos, B2B productions, and an award-winning feature-length documentary.

I've directed or contributed to 80+ productions, working with a local production company to shoot and produce ads for over 40 businesses, helping each one stand out and connect with their market. That range, from 24-hour film marathons to national brand commercial work, has sharpened a versatility most single-lane specialists never develop: I can walk onto a set as director, step behind the camera as cinematographer, or sit down in the edit bay, and deliver at a professional level in any of those roles.

Watch the reels below for a curated look at both sides of that work: video production and creative direction.

That independent filmmaking background is also what shapes my client work. The instinct for story, pacing, and audience connection I built directing narrative film is the same instinct I bring to a YouTube channel, a product launch video, or a brand campaign, because good storytelling follows the same rules no matter who's paying for it.
