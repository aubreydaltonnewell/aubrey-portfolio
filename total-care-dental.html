About the Project

I work with the marketing team to create the brand image for Total Care Dental & Wellness through content across video, ads, social media — including Instagram and Facebook — website, and graphics, so every touchpoint feels consistent and true to the practice's voice.

That work spans print materials and live event presence, alongside the day-to-day video and social content, all built around one goal: helping the market see and trust the brand. That consistent, full-scope presence has helped the practice expand and grow.
