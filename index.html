About

About Me

I've been telling stories through a camera for most of my life. Inspired early by filmmakers such as Alfred Hitchcock and Steven Spielberg, I began casting family and friends in short films made with my father's Hi8 camcorder. By age 14, my first festival film had screened at five regional festivals. At 16, another short earned second place at the Kiwanis Teen Film Festival. Those early experiences gave me more than a love of filmmaking. They taught me how a strong idea, clearly told, can connect with an audience.

Over the past two decades, I've contributed to more than 80 productions as a creative director, producer, director, cinematographer, writer, editor, and camera operator. My work has included commercials, short films, music videos, live events, corporate productions, major broadcast content, and an award-winning feature-length documentary. Along the way, I've helped businesses, universities, nonprofits, healthcare organizations, and consumer brands communicate what makes their work meaningful.

For the past seven years, I've led creative and marketing work for Total Care Dental & Wellness, Total Care Kids, and Living Well with Dr. Michelle. I've helped shape brand identities, build content strategies, launch products, develop websites, create advertising campaigns, and establish an in-house production studio. That work helped grow a YouTube channel from under 1,000 subscribers to more than 200,000, generating 31.8 million views and over 856,000 hours of watch time.

What I bring to every project is the ability to see both the larger creative vision and the details required to deliver it. I can develop the strategy, shape the message, lead the team, direct the production, step behind the camera, and guide the work through post-production. Whether I'm creating a brand campaign, product launch, educational series, or independent film, my goal remains the same: tell a clear, compelling story that connects with people and moves them to act.
