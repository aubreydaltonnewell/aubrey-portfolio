About

The Person Behind the Camera

Hi, I'm Aubrey Dalton Newell. With over two decades of experience in media production, I lead video, photography, and brand content for a multi-brand healthcare and wellness organization — creating everything from patient education videos to full campaign content across social channels.

What I love most is helping share a message that matters: that answers can be found for people's health and wellness. Being part of that story is what makes this work meaningful to me.

Outside of client and brand work, I'm an independent filmmaker with an award-winning background — competition wins and festival selections for short films and commercial work shaped how I think about story, pacing, and audience connection in everything I direct.
